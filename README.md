### About

The experience came before the degree, not after it. I spent three years at Computer Technology
Corporation building REST APIs for an outbound SMS, email and voice campaign platform, decomposing a
monolithic staffing system into Spring Boot microservices, and shipping one product to iOS and Android
from a single Flutter codebase.

I owned the quality side of that work too — JUnit 5 and Mockito past **85% coverage**, SonarQube and
Fortify wired into the SDLC, and **10+ Grafana dashboards** that cut how long production issues went
unnoticed by roughly 30%.

I like backend systems where the correctness is checkable: layered services, a real test suite, and a
pipeline that will not let a bad build through.
